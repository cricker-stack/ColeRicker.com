Chicago Fire FC marketing plan

Marketing strategy, Fall 2025 · Columbia College Chicago

A full marketing plan built around one problem: the Fire can fill Soldier Field for big matches but struggles to bring fans back for the mid-tier games. The plan makes matchdays a social event and goes after Latino communities, families, students, and digital-first Gen Z fans.

+15%home attendance goal
+25%Instagram and TikTok followers
+18%merchandise sales
$58,500fully budgeted tactics
  • Researched the market with SWOT and PESTLE analysis, then segmented audiences by geography, demographics, psychographics, and behavior.
  • Built a tactical calendar: local influencer partners, monthly neighborhood pop-ups, themed match nights, abandoned-cart emails, and limited-run merch designed with Chicago artists.
  • Set up monthly management reviews and quarterly check-ins so every goal has a metric behind it.
